§1 About

I'm Joshua Stough, a computer scientist and teacher at Bucknell University. I teach systems courses, from logic gates through operating systems, and electives in image processing, computer vision and machine learning. My research applies machine learning to medical images, most recently ultrasound and histopathology, usually with undergraduate collaborators. Please see my Bucknell page for more.
