About

I'm Joshua Stough, a computer scientist and teacher. At Bucknell University I teach systems, from logic gates up to operating systems, and electives in image processing, computer vision and machine learning. My research applies machine learning to medical images, most recently ultrasound, usually with undergraduate collaborators.
