Three directions

Prototypes for joshuastough.github.io as a personal hub. Every page has the same words and links; only the design differs. They're on a local branch and marked noindex. Each follows your system's light or dark setting.

  • A · Swiss grid

    Huge type on a strict grid, black and white plus one orange. The butterfly becomes a duotone, and a numbered index replaces the pills and cards.

    Hover a row of the index.

    OpenLightDark

  • B · Terminal

    The page is a shell session: fully readable without JavaScript, and typeable with it. The butterfly comes out as ASCII art, with its orange spots kept.

    Type help, press Tab to complete, or try sudo.

    OpenPaperPhosphorWith help

  • C · Image lab

    The butterfly is live. Sample, quantize, blur, find edges, shift hue or dither it in the browser, then drag to compare. Each operation links to its chapter in the book.

    Drag across the picture, or use the arrow keys on the divider.

    OpenLightDarkDither

Preview flags: ?theme=dark or ?theme=light on any page; ?op=edges&v=4&wipe=0.3 on the lab; ?run=help,ls on the terminal.
